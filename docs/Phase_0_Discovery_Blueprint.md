# Phase 0: Discovery & Blueprint

**Duration:** 3–5 days

## Goal
Understand the owner's actual workflow before writing a single line of code.

## Activities
- Walkthrough of current process (register, Excel, WhatsApp groups)
- Map existing batches, fee structures, staff roles
- Define success metrics (e.g. "reduce fee follow-up calls by 80%")

## Deliverable
A one-page **Solution Blueprint** with wireframes — demonstrates business understanding, not just technical intent.

## Why It Matters
Most freelancers skip this step. Doing it first positions you as a consultant, not just a coder.
